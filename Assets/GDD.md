# Humans
#### Movement
All models should be able to move around. Movement should cost energy based on the terrain they are walking on — muddy ground and stony ground cost more energy than plain ground.

#### Food Status
Food gives models energy and health. Different kinds of food can give different amounts of energy. If the food status becomes low, energy drains faster, health also decreases, and finding food becomes the top priority.

#### Energy Status
Energy allows models to move, look for, and gather resources. If a model's energy is low, it moves slower. If energy is completely drained, the model instantly falls asleep wherever it is. Sleep and food restore energy, depending on the food and the quality of sleep.

#### Different Personalities
Models will have different priorities. For example:
- **Lazy:** Will eat the nearest food, moves slowly, and keeping energy is the top priority.
- **Greedy:** Will gather the most food, materials, and everything else, even if all statuses are full.
- **Thief:** Will steal from other models' chests. It's a combination of lazy and greedy personalities and will steal even if it doesn't need to.
- **Adventurer:** Likes to travel long distances and has a larger amount of energy and sight for exploring.

#### Sleep
If a model's energy is low, it can sleep and regain energy.

#### Sleep Quality
The place where a model sleeps restores different amounts of energy. For example, sleeping for 1 hour on cold, hard terrain restores only 50% of energy, while warm, plain ground restores up to 90%.

#### Food Search
If a model gets hungry, energy drains faster, so it should look for food or eat what it has saved.

#### Sight Limit
A model should be able to see 15 m ahead of itself. At night or in dark environments, the sight limit drops to 7 or 5 m.

#### Swimming
Some models are natural swimmers, but other models can learn to swim too — though it costs energy.

#### Health
A model's health can be affected by food, energy, and other dangers (to be added later).

#### Roles
Later, once they form a community, models can take on different roles: workers who gather resources, hunters who gather food, builders who build houses and other buildings, etc.

#### Gender (maybe?)
Optional, but models can have genders that affect how they perform — or maybe not. It could be conceptual, with various roles available to both genders.

#### Mating (maybe?)
For reproduction purposes.

# World

#### Food Spawn System
Food can spawn randomly, or we can have a system that spawns chosen food at a location we select.

#### Food Variety
We can have different kinds of food that provide varying amounts of energy and satiety: berries, fish, meat (from hunting), apples, etc.

#### Water
We can place lakes, seas, or rivers on the map. Swimmers can cross them, but we can also use them to set borders so models don't spread across the map.

#### Water Placing System
A system that places water where we choose — for changing borders or even creating challenges for the models.

#### Temperature
Temperature changes throughout the day — cold nights and hot days. Cold drains more energy, and cold spots affect sleep quality.

#### Materials
Later in the game, when models take on roles, builders need materials to build. Wood, stone, and iron (maybe later) can be used to build buildings and tools.

#### Rain
Rain decreases temperature, makes terrain muddy, or can even cause floods and create new lakes or rivers.

#### Weather
Whether a day is hot, cold, rainy, snowy, windy, etc.
